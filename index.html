Hello, I'm

Tiwari

I build numerical methods for fluid flows and moving interfaces.

I'm a PhD researcher at IIT Ropar working at the meeting point of numerical analysis and fluid mechanics. My work covers finite element and discontinuous Galerkin discretisations of PDEs, phase-field models of multiphase flow, and the instabilities that shape interfaces in confined geometries.

I care about schemes that respect the physics — conserving mass, dissipating energy the way the continuous model does — and about making them fast enough to use, through careful solver and preconditioner design in Firedrake and PETSc.

Research

My research sits between theory and implementation: designing discretisations with provable structure, then building and testing them on hard multiphase problems.

Conservative diffuse-interface methods

Phase-field formulations that keep each fluid's volume exactly conserved while keeping interfaces sharp and bounded. I implement and benchmark these in Firedrake on standard interface-transport and two-phase flow tests.

Phase-fieldMass conservationBenchmarks

Structure-preserving DG for Cahn–Hilliard

Mixed discontinuous Galerkin schemes for Cahn–Hilliard-type equations that inherit mass conservation and discrete energy dissipation from the continuous model.

Mixed DGEnergy stabilityGradient flows

Hele-Shaw flows and interfacial instability

Diffuse-interface models of flow in thin gaps, where viscosity contrasts drive fingering. I study how the numerics influence the onset and growth of these patterns.

Hele-ShawViscous fingeringStability

Spinodal decomposition

Linear growth rates and early-time dynamics of phase separation, comparing analytical dispersion relations with fully nonlinear simulations.

Linear stabilityGrowth rates

Pseudospectral solvers

Fourier pseudospectral methods for periodic phase-field problems, used both as fast solvers and as high-accuracy references for finite element results.

Spectral methodsFFT

Parameter-robust solvers

Block preconditioners and solver configurations whose performance holds up as physical parameters, interface width and mesh size change.

PreconditioningPETScRobustness
